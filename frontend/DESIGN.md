## Overview

The visual system follows a MiroFish-inspired white, black and orange vocabulary: a black horizontal masthead, a strong sans title, a quiet workflow column and an input console. These principles are implemented without copying reference code, logos or illustrations. It is an Operate interface: product input and report conclusion lead.

## Colors

White owns the page. Black owns the masthead, headings, numbers and primary actions. Orange signals the current step and leading strategy; darker orange supplies accessible link text. Pale orange marks conditional findings. No blue navigation rail or notebook panel remains.

## Typography

System fonts only. The frontmatter enumerates responsive size steps; the tonal sidecar records the authored neutral and warning ramps. Main titles are 40–52px on desktop and 30px on mobile. Form labels are at least 14px, secondary text at least 12px, mobile inputs 16px. Tabular numerals carry measurements; monospace is limited to identifiers and real sequence numbers.

## Layout

A horizontal masthead and workflow replace the sidebar. Home pairs a three-step guide with one focused launch console: known-audience campaign or broad market discovery. Product, price, virtual media budget and campaign ages are essential inputs. One primary action creates the study and starts its simulation. Optional sources and numerical assumptions expand progressively; manual preparation, keywords, stock and provider options remain in advanced settings. Examples require an explicit click; a new product starts neutral.

Reports lead with a plain-language verdict, a named scenario's sales/revenue/spend/CAC, and a ranked comparison of exactly three campaigns. Baselines, uncertainty, channels, trajectories and reproduction data remain below in labelled disclosures. Page analysis first explains findings, sampled age groups and questions, followed by progressive evidence and individual reviews.

Market discovery starts with tentative age/channel conclusions, source coverage, competitor references, switching and barriers. Missing evidence stays unknown; all-zero outcomes remain inconclusive. Ordinary product caveats remain in the numerical report, while unsubstantiated or unevaluated claims stay prominent before market conclusions.

## Elevation & Depth

Borders and white space replace decorative shadows. Type, alignment, sequence and selective orange supply hierarchy.

## Shapes

Rectangular surfaces, small control radii and native disclosures follow the explicit reference. Charts use real values and icons use Phosphor.

## Components

Primary actions state their result. Failed numeric runs expose an explicit resume button. An undefined CAC means not calculable, never free. Credibility is visible: old reports are unevaluated; extraordinary claims without evidence are unsubstantiated, never verified or proven fraudulent.

## Do's and Don'ts

Preserve every function and stored user datum. Never add alternative scenarios into a fictitious overall total. Distinguish highest virtual revenue from a statistically supported winner. Keep source evidence, provider consent, costs and traces accessible. Never infer population validity from bounded synthetic personas.
