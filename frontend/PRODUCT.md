# Spill the Market

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Creators and entrepreneurs testing an idea locally before committing to a launch.

## Product Purpose

Compare three price and communication strategies against no-campaign baselines with reproducible individual simulations and bounded representative profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Five modules: research, personas, media plans, virtual purchases, and local product-page capture with optional visual analysis. Adults only. English/French. Fictional examples are explicitly selected. All displayed numerical results come from the API. API spending and virtual media spending are separate. No real campaigns or purchases. Remote page analysis requires explicit consent and a configured provider. No remote fonts or trackers. Local API session token required for writes.

## Brand Commitments

User-directed redesign: MiroFish-inspired white, black and orange console, independently implemented without copied reference code or assets. System typography and Phosphor icons. A focused product entry followed by clear, assumption-bound reports; detailed traces stay available below the primary conclusions.

## Evidence on Hand

Canonical contracts: ../docs/INTERFACES.md and ../src/spill_market/models.py. Product scope: ../PLAN-A-VALIDER.md. Demo products and research are explicitly fictional, not market proof.

## Product Principles

Evidence stays attached to claims. Simulation uncertainty is conditional on assumptions. Every active control performs its named action. User-selected age bounds remain explicit.
