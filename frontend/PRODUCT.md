# Spill the Market

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Creators and entrepreneurs testing an idea locally before committing to a launch.

## Product Purpose

Compare three price and communication strategies against no-campaign baselines with reproducible individual simulations and bounded representative profiles.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Four modules: research, personas, media plans, virtual purchases. Adults only. English/French. Offline fictional examples. All displayed numerical results come from the API. API spending and virtual media spending are separate. No real campaigns or purchases. Page audit is future work. No remote fonts or trackers. Local API session token required for writes.

## Brand Commitments

Approved brief: expressive blue, ink and white launch notebook; system typography and Phosphor icons. An Operate interface, designed for completing an experiment.

## Evidence on Hand

Canonical contracts: ../docs/INTERFACES.md and ../src/spill_market/models.py. Product scope: ../PLAN-A-VALIDER.md. Demo products and research are explicitly fictional, not market proof.

## Product Principles

Evidence stays attached to claims. Simulation uncertainty is conditional on assumptions. Every active control performs its named action. User-selected age bounds remain explicit.
