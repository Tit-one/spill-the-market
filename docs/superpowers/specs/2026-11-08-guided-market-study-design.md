# Guided campaigns and market exploration

The user asks for an intuitive launch in a few clicks and a genuine market-study workflow: either a known target, or exploratory age/channel comparison, existing alternatives, switching and foreseeable barriers. Preserve the MiroFish-inspired visual identity and existing saved studies/page analysis.

## Approach

Use two guided objectives over the existing local research, numerical population and worker system. This reuses actual runs and provenance, instead of adding an explanatory facade over incomplete trace samples. Keep the advanced laboratory available through disclosures. Automatic paid web search would require new spend authorization; guided launch uses offline inputs or explicitly supplied public URLs with no provider calls. Existing explicit, budgeted Tavily research remains available.

## Product flow

Choose **known-target campaign** or **market exploration**; enter name, description/category, price and a virtual media budget; launch. Known target exposes strict ages; exploration uses a declared broad adult range and compares intersected conventional age bands. Optional product/competitor URLs and geographic context enrich the study. Population1000,30days and deterministic numerical defaults are displayed concisely and editable in advanced options. No fabricated example content is preloaded.

The report first answers what to try, for whom, on which channels and what might prevent adoption. Segment/channel recommendations remain conditional hypotheses and can be inconclusive. A competitor URL is an identified reference, not automatically a verified competing product; source excerpts retain provenance. Missing research produces a visible research gap. Do not infer market size, installed user bases or real switching rates from a page or an age prior.

## Numerical contract

`StudySpec.objective` defaults to `campaign`; optional/defaulted `market` contains incumbent_share0.6, switching_friction0.5, addressable_populationnull, product_url and competitor_urls. Only exploration enables declared switching assumptions. Existing-solution membership uses a separate seeded stream; individual friction reduces adoption on all acquisition routes. Switchers are a subset of buyers, never additional units. Checkpoints persist membership/attribution and reject incompatible assumptions.

Age aggregates cover every numerical individual, with exact per-replica population/buyer/revenue reconciliation. Conventional bands18–24,25–34,35–44,45–54,55–64,65–100 are clipped to the submitted range. Channel rows retain actual attribution and unique-reach denominators. Summary means and intervals use validation replicas. Existing campaign response mathematics remain unchanged.

`report.market_analysis` organizes sourced comparison excerpts, reported difficulties, numerical age/channel results, switching counts, declared extrapolation only when the user supplies a market population, and actionable research gaps. Export it as well as displaying it. Rule-based credibility screening remains explicitly unvalidated; module5 readers still receive only their current visible page context.

## Verification

Test guided launch with no URLs, public-source failures and no paid-provider dispatch; backward-compatible saved studies; single-age/boundary and full-population reconciliation; switching containment/zero-share/zero-friction; exact resume; ordinary legacy projections; both UI journeys and advanced access. Inspect actual desktop/mobile ready states and preserve user data. Requalify the new package rather than distributing the previously qualified wheel.
