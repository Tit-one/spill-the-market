# Guided campaigns and market studies

Choose **known-target campaign** when strict adult ages are already decided, or **market exploration** to compare age bands/channels and investigate alternatives, switching and adoption barriers. Supply product name, description, category, price and virtual media budget, then launch. Opening an existing study never reruns it.

## Evidence

Guided launch saves a study, fetches explicitly supplied public product/competitor URLs as text, proposes three product-adapted plans and queues a numerical worker. No paid search or inference is implicit. Failed/limited collection retains editable data and shows a warning. No-source output is labelled hypotheses-only; fictional demonstrations stay separate.

Broader automatic web discovery requires the separate **Sources / research** action with configured Tavily, verified prices and a positive real API budget. Market queries include alternatives, competition, pricing, reviews and migration. Identical requests retain their cache identity. Search snippets and external statements are not independently verified facts or representative surveys.

Competitor URLs identify references, not verified competitive relevance. Excerpts retain source IDs, URLs and dates; the tool invents no companies when evidence is missing. Public links redact common credential/tracking fields. A separate opaque requested-URL identity preserves association between distinct product IDs and through redirects. Old sources without sufficient identity can remain unlinked instead of borrowing another page's content.

## Ages and channels

Every numerical person contributes to one clipped band:18–24,25–34,35–44,45–54,55–64 or65–100. Ages are uniformly sampled within the submitted strict range, not census-weighted. Population, purchases and integer revenue reconcile in each replica. Purchase attribution persists after recency windows expire.

Validation summaries show means, denominator-labelled rates and replica dispersion. Sparse/zero/tied responses and overlapping available intervals produce an inconclusive recommendation. Suggested targets are conditional leads. Channel budgets, creatives and exposure opportunities differ, so these rates are not isolated causal tests of every possible channel.

## Switching

Only market discovery enables switching. Defaults `market.incumbent_share=0.6` and `market.switching_friction=0.5` are illustrative editable priors, not inferred from websites or age. Independent seeded streams share membership/friction across strategies. Friction reduces incumbent gains and both purchase hazards; zero friction or zero incumbents preserves original campaign equations.

Switchers and new-to-category buyers partition simulated purchases. Retained incumbents are assumed existing-solution users who did not buy. Counts pool all existing alternatives; they are not named competitors' measured churn or captured accounts. Campaign mode leaves those unmodelled cohort counts null.

Market population defaults to unknown. If the owner supplies `addressable_population`, projected switchers are simply `simulated_switchers / simulated_population × supplied_population`, labelled **unvalidated extrapolation**. This adds no units or revenue to the numerical run.

## Act on the report

The overview leads with target/channel leads or an inconclusive result, numerical switching and evidence coverage, then barriers, competitor references and next actions. Unsupported extraordinary promises can add a credibility barrier, without establishing fraud. Open **La page / Page** for screenshot-grounded comprehension, questions and trust by persona; those findings do not automatically change numerical sales.

Validate important findings with real prospect interviews and a small measured campaign. JSON preserves the full report; CSV includes the market object and per-scenario switchers. HTML/Markdown contain readable market sections and standalone source references. External statements and simulated hypotheses retain distinct statuses.
